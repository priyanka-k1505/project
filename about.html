About the project

Comparing deep-learning models for kidney stone detection

A study of four architectures on kidney ultrasound images, and the adaptive graph model that came out on top.

This project compares deep-learning approaches for detecting kidney stones in ultrasound images: a Vision Transformer (ViT-B/16), MobileNetV3 + GCN, InceptionNeXt + GCN, and a new Adaptive Similarity GCN with an EfficientNetV2 backbone (ASGCN-EffNetV2).

The baseline GCN models connect every image patch to every other patch. ASGCN-EffNetV2 instead builds a k-nearest-neighbour graph from the cosine similarity of patch features, so related regions, such as a stone boundary and the tissue around it, are linked dynamically.

Models were trained on a stratified 70 / 15 / 15 split. Checkpoints were chosen on the validation set only, and every reported metric comes from a separate test set the models never saw during training.
